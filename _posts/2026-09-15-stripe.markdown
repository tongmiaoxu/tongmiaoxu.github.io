We present a real-to-sim policy evaluation framework that combines mesh-based scene reconstruction in MuJoCo, calibrated robot dynamics, and STRIPE, a learned image translator that maps simulated renders to real-camera appearance while preserving spatial layout. Across four manipulation tasks and four policy families (ACT, Diffusion Policy, GR00T N1.5, π0.5), STRIPE gives stronger sim-to-real success-rate correlation and policy rank agreement than raw rendering, classical color alignment, or Pix2Pix.
