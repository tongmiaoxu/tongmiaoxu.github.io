Imitation-learned policies break when the camera view or object placement changes. CORE decouples vision-based decision making from robot execution with a contact-anchored canonical object frame: point clouds and end-effector actions are expressed in that frame, so one policy generalizes across camera viewpoints, object placements and robot kinematics. On RLBench door opening it reaches 95% success on unseen placements, versus 30% for ACT and 15% for DP3.
